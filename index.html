About Aerial Views

Aerial Views was founded by Arjun Aranha, a DGCA Certified Remote Pilot for Small and Medium drones, with a passion for capturing places, properties and meaningful moments from a fresh perspective.

We work with businesses, property owners, event teams and creative partners to plan drone shoots around their goals. From the first conversation to flight planning and capture, we focus on clear communication, suitable planning and visuals that serve your purpose.
